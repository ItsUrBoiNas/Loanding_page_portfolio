# Phase 1: Perfect Text and Sharp Contrast

In this phase, we will fix colors and sizes using strict design rules. Everything will look incredibly clean and easy to read.

### Exactly what we will do in `naslogic.html` and the CSS:

- **Hero Text Color:** On line 854, I will change the text to a crisp, slightly softened white (`color: rgba(255,255,255,0.9);`). It will be perfectly readable without glaring at the user.
- **Second Hero Button:** On line 861, I will make the "Get a Free Quote" button a clean, minimal outline button. When you hover over it, it will fill with a solid color instantly and sharply. No blurry glass, just sharp code.

- **Review Sizes:** On lines 980, 997, and 1015, I will make the words bigger (`font-size: 1.2rem;`) and fix the spacing between the lines so it reads like a printed magazine.
- **Pricing Buttons:** On line 1104, I will change the $2,500 plan button to a solid, flat green color (`background: var(--accent);`). It will match the brand perfectly and look highly professional.
- **Sale Ending Text:** On line 1138, I will make the text bigger (`font-size: 1.2rem;`) and color the numbers in a clean, solid gold so the price stands out firmly.
- **Industry Tags:** On lines 1181 to 1215, I will increase the padding (`padding: 24px 24px;`) and give them a sharp 1-pixel border. When you hover, the border will change color instantly. Clean and simple.
