What Makes a Landing Page Convert?

Most landing pages fail because they try to do too much. The ones that work have five things: a headline that talks to one specific person, one clear call-to-action (not six), real proof from real people, a design that loads fast on a phone, and something that kills the "what if it sucks" fear — like a money-back guarantee.

That's the playbook we use at Naslogic. We build pages for roofers, dentists, HVAC guys, lawyers, and restaurant owners — businesses where the phone ringing is the whole point. Most of our clients see their conversion rate double or triple within the first month.
